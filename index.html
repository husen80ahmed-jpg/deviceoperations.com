01 / The Domain Thesis

One name. The whole device lifecycle.

Unlike narrow feature names, Device Operations encompasses the workflows that keep distributed products functioning after they leave the factory. That breadth makes the domain relevant across multiple platform categories.

01

Zero-Touch Provisioning

Connect, enroll, configure and activate new devices at scale, reducing manual onboarding work.

02

Fleet Orchestration

Coordinate configuration, policy and device state across customers, sites and hardware types.

03

Secure OTA Updates

Manage firmware and software rollouts, targeting, monitoring and recovery across live fleets.

04

Remote Remediation

Investigate issues, run repeatable workflows and minimize avoidable on-site interventions.

05

Lifecycle Intelligence
